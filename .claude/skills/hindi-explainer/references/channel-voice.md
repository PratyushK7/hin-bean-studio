# Channel voice: Hindi Explainer Studio

## Positioning

A serious but accessible Hindi explainer channel for curious viewers. It explains how the world works through evidence, clear reasoning and visual storytelling. Its identity comes from clarity and trust, not a mascot or a catchphrase.

## Voice

- Calm, intelligent and conversational.
- Curious without being breathless.
- Serious without being gloomy.
- Precise without sounding academic.
- Confident where the evidence is strong, transparent where it is limited.
- A human narrator speaking to one thoughtful viewer.

## Language

Use spoken Hindi in Devanagari. A natural sentence may contain familiar English technical nouns, but the grammar should remain natural Hindi.

Preferred:
- "लेकिन असली वजह कुछ और है।"
- "इसे समझने के लिए पहले एक छोटी-सी बात देखिए।"
- "यहाँ एक ज़रूरी फर्क है।"
- "इसका मतलब यह नहीं कि..."
- "उपलब्ध आँकड़े एक अलग तस्वीर दिखाते हैं।"

Avoid:
- "दोस्तों, आज हम आपको बताने वाले हैं..."
- "आपके होश उड़ जाएंगे!"
- "दुनिया का सबसे बड़ा और सबसे खतरनाक रहस्य..."
- "100% गारंटी", "हर कोई", "हमेशा", "कभी नहीं" unless literally supported.
- Excessive "दरअसल", "असल में", "मतलब", "तो दोस्तों" and filler transitions.

## Sentence rhythm

- One idea per sentence.
- Alternate concise lines with occasional explanatory sentences.
- Use pauses at conceptual turns.
- Avoid a long series of rhetorical questions.
- Define jargon once, then use the same term consistently.

## Endings

End with a conclusion that answers the central question and gives the viewer a useful mental model. Do not append a generic subscription request to every script. A restrained channel sign-off may be used when it fits the format.
