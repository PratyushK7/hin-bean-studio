# Hindi explainer Shorts

Use the master `hindi-explainer` skill and `hindi-script` skill first.

## Format

- 9:16 vertical.
- Target 30–60 seconds; use the actual subject and narration to decide.
- One central question, one explanatory mechanism and one payoff.
- Make the title promise specific and deliver it within the video.
- Do not cram a long-form argument into a rapid fact list.

## Structure

1. **Opening (0–3 s):** a direct question, counterintuitive observation or concrete stake.
2. **Context (3–10 s):** enough grounding to make the question meaningful.
3. **Explanation (10–40 s):** show the mechanism, comparison or evidence in clear visual steps.
4. **Payoff (final 5–10 s):** answer the question and state the implication accurately.

Adjust these intervals to the actual runtime. They are editorial guidance, not rigid timestamps.

## Visual approach

Choose 5–10 clear scenes or beats. Use a strong visual metaphor only when it maps accurately to the mechanism. Prefer animated diagrams, maps, data, timelines, comparisons and cutaways. Use sourced images if the real-world visual provides evidence or irreplaceable context.

## Production

- Research and cite the claims before scripting.
- Draft five hooks and choose the strongest accurate one.
- Write natural Hindi narration in Devanagari.
- Record or generate a Hindi-capable voice track and verify its duration.
- Align narration to the actual audio; never infer Hindi timings from English word-per-second estimates.
- Create a storyboard before writing animation code.
- Run the renderer's project check and inspect a contact sheet.
- Verify captions, Devanagari glyphs, safe areas, and the title/thumbnail promise.
- Upload nothing until the user explicitly approves it.

## Deliverables

`research.md`, `sources.md`, `claims.json`, `script-hi.md`, `script.json`, `storyboard.json`, renderer scene source, Hindi `.srt`, thumbnail and YouTube upload sheet.
