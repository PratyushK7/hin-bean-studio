# Pronunciation notes

- UPI: say each letter — "You-Pee-Aye".
- NPCI: "En-Pee-See-Aye".
- IMPS: "I-M-P-S".
- PSP: "P-S-P"; expand once as Payment Service Provider.
- Remitter: "ri-mit-er" (sender-side).
- Beneficiary: "ben-uh-fish-uh-ree" (receiver-side).
- QR: "Q-R".
- Reconciliation: "ree-kon-sil-ee-ay-shun".
- Keep technical nouns in English; don't over-pronounce every acronym.
- Pause briefly after "Step one", "Step two", etc.
