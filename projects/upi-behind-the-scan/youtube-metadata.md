## Title
UPI Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? Aapke ₹20 Ke Peeche Kaunse Systems Hain?

## Description
Aap QR scan karte ho, UPI PIN enter karte ho, aur kuch seconds mein payment successful. Lekin is simple tap ke peeche app, PSP bank, NPCI ka UPI network, sender bank aur receiver bank kaise kaam karte hain?

Is Hinglish explainer mein hum ek normal bank-linked UPI payment ko step by step follow karte hain — QR scan se lekar PIN authorization, account debit, receiver credit aur pending transaction tak.
